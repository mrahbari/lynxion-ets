```

- Capital-protective
- Regime-adaptive
- Drawdown-aware
- Correlation-aware
- Statistically defensible

Tasks:

1. Analyze the current risk model behavior and identify:
   - Where risk is underestimated.
   - Where risk is over-constrained.
   - Where risk is disconnected from evidence.

2. Redesign the risk model to include:
   - Regime-conditional risk limits
   - Correlation-cluster exposure control
   - Drawdown-based risk decay
   - Volatility-normalized exposure

3. Propose formulas or pseudocode for:
   - Dynamic max exposure
   - Per-trade risk scaling
   - Portfolio risk gating

4. Explain how each change improves:
   - Capital survival
   - Long-term expectancy
   - Stability during stress regimes

5. Deliver final output as:
   - Architecture description
   - Decision flow
   - Risk control table
```
