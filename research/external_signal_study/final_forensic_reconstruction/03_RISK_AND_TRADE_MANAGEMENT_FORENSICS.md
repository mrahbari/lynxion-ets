# Risk, geometry, and trade management

`evidence/geometry_evidence.csv` distinguishes V2 field availability from primary-verified geometry. V2 contains 142 entries, 136 stops, 88 TP1 fields, and 72 entry+stop+TP1 rows; 64 complete rows are in BOMBICLUB, eight in RADARX_TRANSITION, none in 1K_EVENT. Four older complete text examples were selectively raw-verified (SOL, SAND, WIF, TRX). These counts do **not** validate image coordinates or an executable initial R:R. Position-box images (232 rows) lack two-tick price calibration. ATR-relative stop/target distances, swing-relative placement, and structural invalidation therefore remain UNKNOWN. The LTC raw/V2 entry conflict further limits bulk numeric geometry.

Stop basis classification: `UNKNOWN` for the system. Numeric stops and variable widths are observed; ONDO structure commentary plus a stop does not prove swing-derived stop placement. LDO says the stop is about three times average and recommends one-third usual size (EV-001801). NOT warns of an approximately 5% stop and leverage at most 4 (EV-001876); ADA describes a 2.3% unlevered stop and light entry (EV-001890). These are three related human/channel observations, not proof of an automated risk-budget formula. Dynamic risk-normalized sizing is `REPEATED_BEHAVIOR` as advice, `UNKNOWN` as sibling-engine implementation. Leverage is variable in this advice; no universal leverage policy is evidenced.

Reliable target-space geometry is too sparse in the latest generation to infer a target-selection mechanism, minimum R:R, or a pre-entry reward-space gate. Older numeric targets establish that targets were published, not why they were chosen. No target distance/ATR or initial R:R population estimate is defensible.

Management evidence resolves the apparent discrepancy between sparse per-signal linkage and broader channel policy. V2 flags management on 37/307 signal rows, while semantic tags include many channel-level management messages that may not attach to a signal. Raw SOL/ONDO follow-ups explicitly close half and move stop to entry (EV-001515/57). AERO first avoids taking partial while moving to breakeven, then later closes half to free capacity (EV-001937/41). A one-R message permits profit-taking and breakeven (EV-002044), but is not a fixed trigger for all trades. Thus partial TP, BE, runner, and manual adjustment are `POST_SIGNAL_OBSERVED` and often `HUMAN_DISCRETION`; a universal automatic rule is unsupported. Exchange receipts and exact fill/stop-amend sequences were not available.

Loss/failure classification is limited by selective follow-up. V2 outcome labels include 135 OPEN among 307 and are not a complete trade ledger; no win-rate or profit estimate is made. There is insufficient reliable process-adherence evidence to conclude whether documented losses disproportionately reflect process deviation. False breakouts, news, tight stops, poor entries, and execution faults remain UNKNOWN unless explicitly documented for an individual event; this audit does not assign them post hoc.
