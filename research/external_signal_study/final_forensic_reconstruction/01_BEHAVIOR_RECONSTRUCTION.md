The pipeline is therefore partly visible, not reconstructable as a deterministic algorithm. The strongest repeated mechanism is risk adjustment when stop width changes. Selectivity and MTF context are plausible but have sparse, mostly discretionary evidence. `1K_EVENT` is a generation label, not a strategy; the late LDO/SEI TFS attribution is direct retrospective commentary, while earlier HMT attribution is interval-level (EV-002023/24). Per-signal HMT attribution remains UNKNOWN.

No step above demonstrates positive expectancy. Outcome follow-ups are selectively documented and cannot serve as a trade denominator.
