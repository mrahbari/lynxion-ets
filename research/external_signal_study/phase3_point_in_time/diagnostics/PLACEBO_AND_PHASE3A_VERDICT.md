# Phase 3A frozen placebo check and final decision

Preregistration `b9ec946` (SHA-256 `96d0f336f9c0e335b61b8d90df81835180647f9c2d85498d210fa1e34c329193`). This is a **pre-event selection-fingerprint** study using a public Binance USDT-perpetual proxy, not a profit or executable-edge test. Telegram outcomes, post-event returns, and broker data were not used.

The deterministic 1/2/3 UTC-day circular shifts used the original case's 15-minute grid slot (floored from its seconds-precision signal time), remained inside its ISO week and UTC four-hour block, and re-applied the ±6-hour same-symbol signal exclusion and complete pre-T symbol/BTC lookbacks. Each shifted case received a *separate* hashed, same-week/block Family-A control draw on distinct dates, with seeds 31001/31002/31003. Cases with fewer than two valid, distinct shifts were excluded from all placebo comparisons. Every calculated feature's latest input candle close was asserted `<=` its own placebo/control event time. The raw Telegram files and future outcomes were not read by this check.

Of 56 originally matched cases, 55 had at least two eligible shifted timestamps. Retained comparisons: 52 cases/22 independent UTC days at +1 day, 52/22 at +2 days, and 53/23 at +3 days. The one invalid case and all exclusions are preserved in [the shift audit](./placebo_shift_audit.csv); the [paired values](./placebo_paired_differences.csv) and [feature comparisons](./placebo_comparisons.csv) are reproducible from `src/check_frozen_placebos.py` and the hashed source manifest. These are descriptive negative controls, not independent confirmatory trials. Placebo sample composition differs from the 56-case primary screen, so the magnitude comparison is not a paired equal-sample causal estimate.

F4 compression's real standardized difference was +0.476. The three placebo magnitudes were +0.044, -0.046, and -0.269: none matched its magnitude. That observation does **not** rescue the primary finding: its frozen BH q=.163 exceeds .10, its power to detect d=.40 was only about 41%, and no independent engine-attributed/OOS confirmation exists. F3's tiny primary magnitude was exceeded by all three placebos; it was not a candidate anyway. No other placebo exceeded its primary feature magnitude.

**Final status: `PHASE3A_INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_LIMITED`; Phase 3B: `NO-GO NOW` / requires a separate decision, not authorized by this screen.** No feature passed the required BH q<=.10 **and** |d|>=.40 primary screen. Five of six dimensions were underpowered; F2 was only marginally powered. Thus a positive fingerprint is not established, but the stringent `PHASE3A_NO_DEFENSIBLE_FINGERPRINT` null verdict is also unsupported. M3 is mathematically unidentifiable under same-week/same-weekday/same-block matching plus ±6-hour exclusion; M2's exact 30-day BTC-volatility-tercile estimator was not frozen and was not invented after opening the screen. Strict directly labelled HMT N=0, two source symbols remained unverified, and the true candidate universe/venue are unknown. No threshold, feature, source, or cohort was changed to pursue F4. Stop this fixed six-feature run; a fresh independently frozen design and unopened evidence would be necessary to revisit it.
