## Approximate power and collection burden

The **design target is 80% power for `d=.40`**, the original practical-effect and adequate-power standard, with the original conservative isolated six-test BH hurdle `alpha=.10/6=.01667` and two-sided testing. Phase 3A's day-cluster null 95% standardized half-width was `0.3554` at 56 cases/23 days. Approximating its clustered standard error as `0.3554/1.96=0.1813`, and assuming comparable cluster size, dispersion, breadth, and `SE ∝ 1/sqrt(N)`, the normal approximation gives

`N ≈ 56 × [((z_(1-.01667/2)+z_.80) × .1813) / .40]^2 ≈ 121 matched cases`,

where the z-sum is approximately `3.236`. The same estimate implies roughly 50 independent UTC-day clusters if the observed 56-case/23-day ratio persists. The observed day-cluster variance was about 1.54 times an IID-case variance, corresponding to only ~36.5 IID-equivalent observations in the 56-case Phase 3A set; 168 controls are **not** independent cases. This is an orientation estimate, **not** a prospective Monte Carlo guarantee: day and symbol clustering together, changing signal intensity, calendar concentration, or 2026 regime shifts could demand more. The frozen Monte Carlo power at N=56 was only 40.75%, consistent with needing materially more data.

Relative to the old 56 cases, this is about **65 additional matched cases** to bring a *combined* sample to 121. But a clean prospective confirmation cannot count the opened 56: it needs **approximately 121 entirely NEW matched future cases**, spanning about **50 NEW independent UTC days** at the same density. A prudent planning range is **120–180 new matched cases and 50–75 independent days**; the upper part allows lower attribution/coverage and stronger clustering, but does not relax the frozen decision threshold or permit optional stopping. Re-estimate *power only* from blinded future feature dispersion at a preregistered checkpoint, never from observed case-control direction, returns, or outcomes. If day/symbol clustered power remains <80%, classify the prospective test data-limited rather than declaring a null.

The observed historical pool had 58 nominal quantitative HMT candidates over roughly 29 calendar days, of which 56 matched. Naively extending that rate suggests ~two calendar months for 121 matched cases, but that projection assumes the same signal rate, venue coverage, engine mix, and availability; none is verified for future collection. If direct HMT labels are required and remain unavailable, **confirmation is not identifiable**, regardless of calendar time. This is why the present operational verdict is `DATA_HUNGRY`, not `FEASIBLE` or a collection deadline promise.

## New cohort, never an extension of Phase 3A

Name: `COMPRESSION_FORWARD_1`. Its prospective boundary is the later of (a) this plan's Git commit timestamp and (b) an independently recorded activation timestamp for an immutable sibling-signal capture. Only first-message signal timestamps **strictly after** that boundary can enter. Do not backfill old messages into the new cohort. Preserve raw future messages privately; record hashes, capture/receive time, original event time, symbol, side, timeframe, engine evidence, and provenance without storing private exports in Git. Collect every eligible future sibling signal, including non-HMT signals for the frozen same-symbol ±6-hour control exclusion; never select cases after viewing F4 or outcomes. Stop/flag uncertain timestamps, delayed feed, missing side/timeframe/engine provenance, or unavailable historical proxy bars rather than imputing them. Report directly labelled HMT separately from any independently documented interval-attributed HMT; interval-only evidence remains attribution-sensitive and cannot be described as direct engine replication.

For each eligible future HMT case, preserve the **same Phase 3A rules**: first linked message time `T`; supported execution timeframe `{15m,1h,4h,1d}` (not 1w); same Binance USDT-perpetual 15m source/instrument declaration; continuously valid pre-T bars; Family-A controls on 15m grid for the same symbol, copied side/timeframe, same ISO week and UTC four-hour block; exclude timestamps within ±6 hours of *any* sibling signal for that symbol; up to three controls from distinct UTC dates by ascending `SHA-256("phase3a-v1|case_id|control_utc")`; discard from primary comparison if fewer than two. Use a stable, immutable new-cohort case ID, not a reused Phase 3A ID. Seal each case/control after its ISO week is complete and the signal-exclusion archive for that week is immutable. Do not use market values or later outcomes for control selection.

Compute F4 **identically** with the frozen 8/32 completed-bar formula, missing-value rule, no extra indicator and no alternative window or threshold. Preserve the six-feature multiple-testing family for the original statistical gate: the other five frozen features may be calculated solely to retain the original BH correction, not searched or promoted as new leads. For F4 use one paired case-minus-mean(valid controls) value per case, pooled robust MAD standardization, 10,000 UTC-day-cluster bootstrap CI, 10,000 UTC-day-cluster sign-flip draws, and BH q across the original six at `q<=.10` plus `|d|>=.40`. Preserve the original breadth and >=80% power requirements (>=40 matched cases, >=20 days, >=10 symbols, >=10 per side), chronological first/second halves each >=10 days with same sign, leave-top-symbol-out >=50% effect magnitude, and every valid 1/2/3-day same-week/block placebo smaller in magnitude. Preserve frozen seeds and deterministic ordering; do not reroll. Report nominal cases, unique event-day clusters, symbol clusters, side/timeframe breadth, attrition, and attribution strata; do not equate signals or controls with independent information.

**Identifiability defect carried forward:** original M3 same-week + same-weekday + same-four-hour-block matching is impossible with ±6-hour signal exclusion. Original M2 required sign retention under a 30-prior-completed-day BTC-volatility tercile but did not freeze an exact estimator or mapping. Neither can be invented from Phase 3A outcomes. This plan does not silently repair or waive them. Before claiming a *fully* replicated original acceptance gate, a separate, prospective, append-only specification must resolve M2 **before any new-cohort F4 contrasts are opened**; M3 remains reported `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` unless a new independent study, not this frozen replication, is approved. If no valid M2 rule is frozen before opening new contrasts, even a primary q/d success is only `PROSPECTIVE_SCREEN_POSITIVE_WITH_UNRESOLVED_M2`, not full confirmation or Phase 3B permission.

## Falsification and Phase 3B gate

Falsify this lead for the **frozen prospective specification** if a sufficiently powered and broad new cohort shows F4's opposite sign, fails its q/effect gate, loses sign across required chronological halves or after top-symbol exclusion, or is matched/exceeded by a valid placebo. A non-significant underpowered result is `INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_LIMITED`, not proof of absence. If future labels/source/timestamps cannot support genuine prospective matching, label `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`; if collection rate cannot reach an informative sample within the practical project horizon, deprioritize as `TOO_SLOW`. Do not change the 8/32 formula or rerun thresholds to rescue it.

**Considering, not starting, Phase 3B requires all of:** a genuinely unopened prospective cohort meeting >=80% clustered power and breadth; direct or transparently stratified engine attribution; frozen F4 positive `q<=.10` across the original six-feature family and `|d|>=.40`; required chronological/symbol/placebo checks; valid pre-data resolution of M2 sign-retention; no leakage or source/provenance failures; and a separate written authorization/preregistration for economic outcomes with realistic costs and OOS rules. Even then this would establish *selection information*, not a profitable Lynxion strategy. Until these conditions hold, no Phase 3B, production Compression implementation, C-11/C-35 cross-confirmation, or additional feature search is justified.
