# Public methodology review — Qullamaggie

2026-10-05. **Methodology only; no Lynxion Edge or V3 inference.** Primary public sources are the author's [three-setup article](https://qullamaggie.com/my-3-timeless-setups-that-have-made-me-tens-of-millions/) and [episodic-pivot study note](https://qullamaggie.com/how-to-master-a-setup-episodic-pivots/). Personal performance claims and chosen charts are not audited, denominator-complete evidence. No stock threshold, stop, holding period or sizing rule is adopted here.

| Topic | Source-derived fact | Lynxion analogy, not a transferred rule | Untested hypothesis / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup taxonomy | The author distinguishes breakouts after prior strength/consolidation, event-driven episodic pivots, and parabolic exhaustion. | Record selection, state, trigger and invalidation as distinct annotations. | Neither stock setup's existence nor examples establish crypto-perpetual prediction. |
| Relative strength and participation | Stock breakouts are selected from prior leaders; event pivots emphasize a catalyst and unusually high volume. | A future *point-in-time* universe rank and contemporaneous volume/turnover could describe selection. | Cross-sectional rank plus participation may contain incremental information, but R22 has not tested it. A 24/7 perpetual market lacks a stock opening auction and earnings-gap analogue by default. |
| Consolidation, breakout, first pullback or failure | The author describes price compression/pullback before breakout and price/VWAP failure in parabolic shorts; his examples are selected charts. | A later protocol could define setup state, failed setup and no-trade states before labeling outcomes. | The exact causal geometry, direction and holding horizon remain scientific choices. Hand-drawn hindsight lines cannot be treated as point-in-time signals. |
| Asymmetry and risk | The article discusses defined invalidation and payoff relative to risk. | Separate predictive setup evidence from an executable fill/cost and portfolio-risk test. | Author-reported payoff/win rates are not independently verified and do not transfer to perpetual funding, spread or slippage. |
| Case-study discipline | The author reports a large personal chart archive and recommends studying many instances. | Freeze observation windows and record all qualifying winners, losers, no-trades, failed setups and no-fills; preserve raw evidence and pre-outcome reasons. | Selected screenshots/retrospective winners create survivorship and hindsight bias; no denominator-complete systematic replication with independently verified costs was established by this bounded review. |

The useful transfer is **research discipline and a vocabulary for falsifiable selection states**, not a stock strategy. A later Lynxion test would need its own crypto-specific information set, complete candidate denominator, source/receipt times, untouched cohort, preregistered outcomes and executable costs. That future choice is not made in this review. Existing C-14/C-15 momentum and V1 must not be relabeled and retested as a new idea.
