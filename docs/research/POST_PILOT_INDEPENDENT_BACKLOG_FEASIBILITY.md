## R23 — public Qullamaggie methodology review

**SOURCE-DERIVED FACT:** Kristjan Kullamägi's own public [three-setup article](https://qullamaggie.com/my-3-timeless-setups-that-have-made-me-tens-of-millions/) distinguishes breakouts, episodic pivots and parabolic moves; his [episodic-pivot study note](https://qullamaggie.com/how-to-master-a-setup-episodic-pivots/) emphasizes studying many cases and contemporaneous structure/catalyst context. His personal profit statements are self-reports, not audited evidence for Lynxion. These are stock-market, often earnings/opening-gap and swing-trading concepts.

**LYNXION ANALOGY:** A disciplined research workflow can separately record universe selection, prior movement, participation, consolidation/pullback, invalidation, non-events and rejected candidates before outcome labeling. This is an analogy about **how to define and sample a setup**, not a transferred crypto signal.

**HYPOTHESIS, NOT TESTED:** Point-in-time selection and setup geometry might matter independently of direction-only prediction in perpetual futures. Nothing in the cited stock methodology establishes that hypothesis. Do **not** import stock numerical screens, holding periods, stops, targets, sizing, self-reported returns or screenshot winners. Systematic replication also risks hindsight chart selection, survivorship, market-opening/catalyst differences and ignoring losing/no-trade examples. Status remains **supporting methodology review complete / no Lynxion Edge claim**.

## R24 — MANUAL_PROSPECTIVE_SETUP_ANNOTATION (design only)

Status remains **INDEPENDENT_LEAD / NOT_YET_TESTED; NOT ACTIVATED**. A later protocol should freeze venue/universe, session windows, candidate inclusion rule, observer responsibilities and capture clock **before** collecting a single candidate. The rule must enumerate *all* qualifying candidates, not only screenshots that look good.

Proposed immutable **observation record** fields: protocol/version/hash; session ID and frozen start/stop; candidate ID; UTC observation and local receipt timestamps; venue/source; symbol and contract; point-in-time universe snapshot/rank reference; contemporaneous price/volume/participation and setup-state raw references; screenshot file/hash; exact human selection reason written before any outcome; trade/no-trade intent if applicable; observer ID; append timestamp; and explicit missing-data flags. Edits are append-only corrections retaining the original record and reason.

Keep a separate, later **outcome-label record** keyed by candidate ID, with later timestamp and outcome provenance. Preserve all disposition categories where applicable: `WINNER`, `LOSER`, `NO_TRADE`, `FAILED_SETUP`, `NO_FILL`, `NO_MOVE`, plus `UNRESOLVED/CENSORED`; these are not all automatically economic labels. Never delete inconvenient observations. No rule, screenshot, or annotation is being collected under this design. Freezing/activating an outcome-bearing study requires a later preregistration and operator scientific decision.
