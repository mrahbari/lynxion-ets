## Decision rule

`CLOSE` means no further work on the *existing* experiment, not proof that its information family can never work. `CONDITIONAL` means a named data/scientific prerequisite must be met before a new experiment. `EXPLORATORY` means existing imperfect data can be analyzed only as hypothesis generation, with complete caveats and no Edge/after-cost claim. `OPERATOR_CHOICE` means the next hypothesis or outcome opening must be selected and frozen by the operator. No Pilot-3, repeat microstructure capture, backfill, pooled pilots, automatic V3, trading-policy change, or live activation follows from this list.

The operator wishes to advance uncertain leads. That is compatible with bounded **outcome-blind** feasibility and, after an explicit scientific choice, a clearly labeled exploratory analysis of existing imperfect data. It is *not* compatible with repeatedly changing the hypothesis until a favorable result appears. Each negative, data-limited or non-identifiable result remains visible.
