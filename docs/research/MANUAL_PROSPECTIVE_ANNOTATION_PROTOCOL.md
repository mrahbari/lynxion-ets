# Manual prospective annotation protocol — inactive design

Status: **DESIGN ONLY; NOT ACTIVATED**. This document is the task-queue deliverable for R24 and adopts the detailed [schema draft](MANUAL_PROSPECTIVE_SETUP_ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL_DRAFT.md) without creating a candidate inclusion rule, observation session, outcome label or Edge study. Pilot-2 remains the only primary measurement phase.

Before collection, an independently committed and hashed activation must specify venue, contemporaneous tradable universe, complete candidate inclusion rule, observer, UTC observation windows, evidence-capture and clock semantics, missing/interrupted-session rules, and the all-candidate denominator. Cancelled and empty sessions remain records. A later outcome-bearing experiment additionally requires its own preregistered label/outcome rules, minimum independent sample, costs and untouched confirmation boundary, plus operator scientific authorization. These choices are **UNMADE**.

Keep four separate append-only records: (1) predeclared session; (2) candidate observed at time T; (3) contemporaneous trade/no-trade/failed-setup/no-fill disposition where applicable; (4) later outcome label, stored separately and inaccessible for rewriting the earlier reason. Candidate records include UTC and receipt timestamps, symbol/venue, point-in-time rank and participation references if available, setup state, original screenshot/raw bytes and SHA-256, contemporaneous human reasoning and missingness flags. Each append records author/time, stable IDs, content hash and previous-hash or manifest lineage. Corrections append a new linked record; they do not overwrite the original. Never delete winners, losers, `NO_TRADE`, `FAILED_SETUP`, `NO_FILL`, `NO_MOVE`, unresolved or inconvenient observations. A no-trade is still a candidate, not an omitted row.

No collection can start under this design because the inclusion rule and observation window remain undefined. `WINNER`/`LOSER` are placeholders for a future separate label protocol, not labels assigned now. Screenshots of selected successes and later chart inspection cannot define the candidate universe after the fact. No authenticated exchange access or orders are authorized.
