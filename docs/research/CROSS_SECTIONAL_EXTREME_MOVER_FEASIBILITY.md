## Candidate frequency and independence

The number of eligible movers, controls, independent UTC days, listing churn, missing-data fraction and clustering are **UNKNOWN**. Neither a top-N nor a percent-move rule exists, so an event count would be invented. Capacity illustration only: 1,000 symbols × 24 hourly slots × 30 days = 720,000 symbol-hour *slots*, not independent opportunities; retention, API limits, concurrent symbols, gaps and storage must be budgeted from a later frozen cadence. Symbol/day and common market moves imply correlated observations. A prospective unopened window could make the universe denominator auditable if snapshots and bars are captured from its start; a historical confirmatory window is not yet qualified. An untouched test cohort would need to be declared separately, before observing outcomes.

## Decision and dependencies

`EXTREME_MOVER_DATA_PARTIAL`: price/participation bars and current listing metadata are available in principle; a complete **historical** time-T perpetual universe and spread/cost panel are not proven. Prospective public Binance collection appears technically possible but is **not activated**. No other venue is chosen by this finding. The next authorized work is an outcome-blind information contract specifying what must be observed at T. A venue, inclusion rule, ranks/thresholds, sample floor, horizon, study activation or Edge test requires a separate scientific decision. Pilot-2 remains the sole active primary measurement phase.
