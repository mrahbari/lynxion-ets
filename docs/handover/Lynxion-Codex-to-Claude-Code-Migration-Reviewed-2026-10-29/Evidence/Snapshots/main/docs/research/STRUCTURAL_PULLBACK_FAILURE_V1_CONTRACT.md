## Fixed parameters and causal primitives

| Parameter | V1 value | Outcome-blind rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pivot width | 2 closed bars on each side | Smallest symmetric confirmed local extremum that requires more than one confirming bar. |
| Prior structure window | 32 contiguous M15 bars ending at anchor confirmation | Bounded local structure and finite memory; not selected from returns. |
| Recovery confirmation deadline | 12 bars after anchor confirmation | Bounded setup lifetime; deliberately a design choice for Checkpoint A. |
| Continuation deadline | 8 bars after recovery-pivot confirmation | Bounded event expiry; deliberately a design choice for Checkpoint A. |

A swing high at index `i` is confirmed only on close of `i+2` if `high[i]` is strictly greater than highs at `i-2,i-1,i+1,i+2`; a swing low is the strict reverse. The original pivot time is `open[i]`; its confirmation and first observation time are `close[i+2]`. No pivot is backdated to `i`. If one bar would qualify as both high and low, discard both for structure. A bar can participate in multiple independently anchored setups. All indices below refer to a single contiguous symbol/M15 segment.

Mechanical interpretation fixed before code: a confirmed pivot enters the available-pivot list only at the close of its rightmost confirming bar. The four prior pivots must have strictly increasing **original** indices and alternating types; their confirmation times must also be no later than anchor confirmation. For each newly confirmed anchor choose the latest eligible four-pivot chain by lexicographically latest original-index tuple `(anchor, preceding pivot, preceding pivot, oldest pivot)`. The 32-bar window includes pivot originals from `anchor_confirmation_index-31` through that confirmation index. Recovery's two consecutive directional close changes are checked on adjacent bars after anchor confirmation and no later than the original P pivot bar. The P original index must exceed anchor confirmation index. P confirmation must occur no later than anchor confirmation +12; equality qualifies. A trigger must be after P confirmation and no later than P confirmation +8; equality qualifies. A level touch invalidates before any same-bar trigger. Distinct P pivots can independently qualify under one anchor; they remain distinct events. A new anchor never silently cancels an older valid pending setup. Invalidated/expired setups never emit. These are interpretations of the approved rules, not additional filters.

## Five bearish rules

1. **Prior directional structure.** At anchor confirmation, find the latest chronological alternating confirmed high `H0`, low `L0`, high `H1`, low `L1` within the 32-bar window, with strict `high(H1)<high(H0)` and `low(L1)<low(L0)`. `L1` is the anchor. Do not use a pivot until its own confirmation close. If multiple eligible quadruples exist, use the one with latest `L1`, then latest `H1`, `L0`, `H0` in that order; each distinct anchor is evaluated separately. Earliest setup observation: `L1` confirmation close.
2. **Countertrend recovery.** After `L1` confirmation, before 12 additional bars close, require at least two consecutive bars whose closes strictly increase versus their immediately preceding closed bars, followed by a confirmed swing high `P` whose original pivot bar is after `L1` confirmation. The two increases must occur between anchor confirmation and `P` pivot inclusive. This is a direction-only qualification; no hidden amplitude or volume threshold. Earliest confirmation: `P` confirmation close.
3. **Confirmed structural reference.** `P` must satisfy the width-2 swing-high rule. Preserve both `P` original open and confirmation close. If `P` cannot be confirmed before the 12-bar deadline, expire. The relevant reclaim level is observed `high(H1)`, frozen when the anchor is formed.
4. **Failure to reclaim.** From the first bar after `L1` confirmation through `P` confirmation, every observed high must remain strictly below `high(H1)`. `high(P)<high(H1)` follows. Reaching or crossing the level invalidates this anchored setup immediately, including on `P` confirmation bar. This is a failed structural reclaim, not a profit claim.
5. **Continuation trigger.** On the first *subsequent* closed bar within 8 bars after `P` confirmation with `close < low(P original pivot bar)`, emit one bearish candidate at that bar's close. A high reaching `high(H1)` on or before the trigger bar invalidates first; same-bar invalidation has priority. If no trigger by deadline, expire. The trigger price is evidence only, not an executable order price.

## Bullish symmetry

Replace the alternating sequence with confirmed low `L0`, high `H0`, low `L1`, high `H1`, requiring `low(L1)>low(L0)` and `high(H1)>high(H0)`; `H1` is anchor. Recovery requires two consecutive strictly decreasing closes and a confirmed swing low `P` after anchor confirmation. Reclaim/failure level is `low(L1)`; every low from after anchor confirmation through trigger must remain strictly above it. The first subsequent closed bar within 8 bars after `P` confirmation with `close > high(P original pivot bar)` is the bullish candidate. A low touching or crossing `low(L1)` invalidates first. All time/deadline and data-quality rules mirror bearish V1.
