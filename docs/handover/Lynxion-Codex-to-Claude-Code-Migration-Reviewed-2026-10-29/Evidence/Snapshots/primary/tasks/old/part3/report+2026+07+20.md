## 3. Key Stress Insights & Tail Risk Protection

1. Extreme Friction Resistance: Fractional Kelly survives extreme fee expansion up to 50  bps roundtrip (Sharpe = 1.04,
   PF = 1.14), whereas Equal Weight degrades to net negative (Sharpe = -0.26, PF = 0.96).
2. Consecutive Losing Streak Protection: Forced 10-consecutive loss streak degrades Equal Weight to net loss (PF = 0.
   90), whereas Fractional Kelly's edge-weighted sizing preserves positive expectancy (PF = 1.04).
3. Tail Loss Risk (CVaR 95%): Fractional Kelly reduces 95% Expected Shortfall to 3.23% (vs 3.41% Equal Weight).
4. Ruin Probability: 0.0% across 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations. Enforcing a 0.25 × Quarter-Kelly fraction provides
   complete mathematical protection against ruin.
