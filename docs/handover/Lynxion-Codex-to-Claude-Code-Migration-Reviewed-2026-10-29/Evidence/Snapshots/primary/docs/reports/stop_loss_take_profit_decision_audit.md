# Stop Loss / Take Profit Decision Audit Report

**Author**: Technical Lead  
**Date**: 2026-07-19  
**Status**: Strategy Layer Decision Audit Complete

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit investigates the mathematical and logical correctness of the stop loss (SL) and take profit (TP) values generated by the Strategy layer for the last two successfully executed trades:
1. **Trade #1**: `BTCUSDT` (BUY) from the `MeanReversion` strategy.
2. **Trade #2**: `SOLUSDT` (BUY) from the `VWAPReversal` strategy.

While our recent remediation successfully aligned stop loss values across all layers (`Strategy = Risk = Execution = Broker = Exchange`), this audit addresses a deeper strategic concern: **Are the strategy-generated stops logically sound, or does a design defect make them overly sensitive to market noise?**

### Core Finding
Both stop losses were extremely tight (**0.457%** for BTC and **0.369%** for SOL) and got hit quickly. The root cause is a **Strategy-level design issue**: the `NGMR_REVERSION` setup formula calculates stop loss distance using a hardcoded percentage of the entry price (`0.5%` below VAL) rather than dynamically adjusting to current market volatility (e.g. using ATR). This results in position sizing distortion and premature stop-outs during periods of standard market noise.

## 3. Hidden Fallbacks Audit

A review of the complete execution path from Strategy to Broker revealed the following fallbacks:

1. **Broker Level Fallback (`multi_broker_service.py` / `broker_execution_service.py`)**
   * **Location**: `MultiBrokerExecutionService._enhance_order_with_risk_parameters`
   * **Trigger**: If the incoming `Order` object has missing (`None`) `stop_loss_price` or `take_profit_price`.
   * **Behavior**: Attempts dynamic SL/TP via `AdvancedRiskManagementService`. If that fails, it falls back to a hardcoded percentage: **2% Stop Loss** and **3% Take Profit** relative to the entry price.
2. **Setup Engine Level Fallbacks (`setup_engine.py`)**
   * **Location**: `SetupEngine.scan_for_setups`
   * **Trigger**: Lack of indicator structures or invalid bounds:
     * **`NGTREND_FOLLOW`**: Falls back to `current_price - 0.01 * current_price` (1% SL) if `val <= 0`.
     * **`NGOI_SETUP`**: Uses hardcoded **1% SL / 2% TP** as default fallback parameter set.
     * **`NGBREAKOUT` / `NGVOLATILITY_BREAKOUT`**: Uses hardcoded **2% TP** as default profit target.

## 4. Risk Consistency & Sizing Math

Dynamic position sizing is calculated using the formula:
$$\text{Position Size (Units)} = \frac{\text{Portfolio Equity} \times \text{Risk Percentage}}{\text{Entry Price} - \text{Stop Loss}}$$

### Mathematical Verification:

#### Trade #1 (BTCUSDT):
* **Expected Risk Amount**: $\$100,000 \times 0.008\text{ (target risk \%)} \times 0.8\text{ (confidence)} = \$640.00$
* **Stop Distance**: $\$64,533.55 - \$64,246.15 = \$287.40$
* **Sizing Calculated Base Size**:
  $$\text{Base Size} = \frac{\$640.00}{\$287.40} = 2.226\text{ units (Notional: }\$143,600.00\text{)}$$
* **Exposure Restriction**: Capped to **0.767043 units** (Notional: $\$49,500.00$) to satisfy the `max_position_exposure` limit ($\$50,000$ limit with $0.99$ safety buffer).
* **Actual Execution Capping**: Enforced down to **0.001549 units** due to the event-level `MAX_ORDER_NOTIONAL_AMOUNT=100` cap.

#### Trade #2 (SOLUSDT):
* **Expected Risk Amount**: $\$100,000 \times 0.008 \times 0.8 = \$640.00$
* **Stop Distance**: $\$76.13 - \$75.85 = \$0.28$
* **Sizing Calculated Base Size**:
  $$\text{Base Size} = \frac{\$640.00}{\$0.28} = 2,285\text{ units (Notional: }\$173,900.00\text{)}$$
* **Exposure Restriction**: Capped to **649.58 units** (Notional: $\$49,500.00$) by the position exposure limit.
* **Actual Execution Capping**: Capped to **1.313543 units** ($100 notional) by the event-level cap.

### Sizing Vulnerability Assessment:
1. **Was the stop distance realistic?** No. A `0.35%` to `0.5%` stop distance is unrealistic for volatile crypto assets. It is highly susceptible to intra-candle market noise.
2. **Did a very tight SL distort sizing?** Yes. The tight stop distances inflated the calculated base sizes to **1.4x to 1.7x** the total portfolio equity. While portfolio limits safely capped them to $\$49,500$, such high base sizes indicate high sizer volatility.
3. **Would a small change significantly alter position sizing?** Yes. Sizing is highly sensitive near zero stop distance. If the SOLUSDT stop was increased by only $\$0.10$ (from $\$0.28$ to $\$0.38$), the base size would decrease by **26%** (to `1684` units).

## 5. Trading Quality Assessment

1. **Was the stop logically positioned?**  
   Conceptually yes. Placing a stop below the Value Area Low (`VAL`) is logical for range-bound mean reversion, as breaking the value area boundary invalidates the range-bound thesis.
2. **Was the stop too close because of market noise?**  
   Yes. Under standard market noise, a `0.35%` or `0.45%` price change occurs regularly within a few candles without signifying a true structural breakout.
3. **Was the strategy timeframe appropriate?**  
   For `MeanReversion`, the `1h` timeframe is appropriate, but the lookback period creates narrow value areas during consolidation. For `VWAPReversal`, the `5m` timeframe is highly noisy, making a tight stop mathematically prone to high stop-out rates.
4. **Would a professional trader consider this SL valid?**  
   No. A professional trader would not place a stop loss exactly at `VAL - 0.5%` without adding a volatility-adjusted buffer (such as `1.5 * ATR`) or spread buffer to insulate against noise-outs.

## 6. Root Cause & Recommendations

### Root Cause Classification
**B) Strategy parameter tuning issue**  
The strategy logic calculates stop loss as a fixed, hardcoded percentage (`0.5%` buffer) below structural indicators rather than scaling it dynamically with asset volatility (ATR).

### Recommended Action
**Strategy Parameter & Logic Optimization**:
* **Update Stop Loss Logic**: Modify `SetupEngine.scan_for_setups` to calculate the stop loss distance using ATR rather than a hardcoded percentage of price:
  $$\text{sl} = \text{val} - (1.5 \times \text{ATR}_{14})$$
* **Implement Minimum Stop Distance**: Add a sanity check in the setup engine to ensure the stop loss distance is at least `1.5%` of the entry price to survive standard market noise.
