# Lynxion Human Observation Inbox

Observations are leads, not facts or strategies. A single observation cannot authorize a candidate
or production change.

```text
Symbol / venue:
Approximate UTC timestamp:
Position side:
What happened before entry:
Maximum favorable excursion:
What happened before giveback or continuation:
BTC/ETH state:
Funding/OI/volume state:
Observed aggressive-flow behavior if available:
Clock/funding boundary proximity:
What appeared unusual:
Why someone may have been forced to trade:
When this observation should fail:
Trade ID / screenshot / journal reference:
```

## خلاصه ساده فارسی

هر تجربه معاملاتی اپراتور ابتدا با این فرم ثبت می‌شود. سپس recurrence، مکانیسم اقتصادی، داده
نقطه‌زمانی، هزینه و شرط ردشدن بررسی می‌شود. تجربه تکی مستقیماً به strategy تبدیل نمی‌شود.
