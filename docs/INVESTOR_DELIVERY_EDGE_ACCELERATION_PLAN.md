## Research-integrity boundary

All controlled investor orders use purpose `INVESTOR_TECHNICAL_DEMO`. Their client/exchange order
identities are stored in an append-only demo registry. Exchange-history reconstruction maps those
identities to the technical-demo label. Both descriptive attribution and prospective edge monitor
exclude the label and report excluded counts. Demo PnL can be shown operationally but can never
enter strategy expectancy, research cohorts, promotion gates, or prospective evidence.

Historical missing values remain missing unless recovered from deterministic authoritative
sources. In particular, no inferred regime, confidence, MFE, MAE, funding, or slippage may be
written back as fact.

## Entry-versus-exit decision model

For each sufficiently supported candidate, measure favorable/adverse path after entry, realized
PnL, MFE capture, giveback, time-to-MFE, time-to-stop, holding duration, and exit reason. Assign
exactly one evidence label:

- `NO ENTRY EDGE`;
- `ENTRY EDGE / EXIT FAILURE`;
- `EDGE PRESENT BUT COST-DESTROYED`;
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.

No breakeven, trailing, profit-lock, or other exit threshold is tuned before this classification.
Any exit alternative is preregistered against a fresh boundary.

## Bounded hypothesis budget

The next cycle may contain at most five independent mechanisms, not five parameter variants.
Candidate definitions must freeze universe, timeframe, side, regime, entry, exit, cost model,
sample rules, temporal split, uncertainty method, and rejection criteria before outcomes open.

The initial lead queue is not an admission decision:

1. causal explanation of the `<=25 USDT` descriptive result;
2. causally defined MeanReversion eligibility;
3. entry quality versus exit failure using reconstructed MFE/MAE;
4. one derivatives/microstructure mechanism only if required inputs have valid provenance;
5. one cross-sectional or cross-venue mechanism only if independent of the preceding candidates.

Leads that cannot obtain a clean definition and unopened boundary are rejected without testing.

## Promotion gate

No strategy reaches production admission without positive net expectancy after realistic costs,
PF above 1, adequate sample, chronological OOS/WFO survival, temporal and appropriate symbol/side
breadth, acceptable concentration and drawdown, positive stress-cost performance, and genuinely
unopened prospective confirmation. Win rate and leverage do not substitute for these gates.
