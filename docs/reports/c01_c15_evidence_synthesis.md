## Earlier Program Evidence That Must Not Be Repeated

The Phase 5–20 campaign already tested a wider set than C-01–C-15:

- Twelve OHLCV strategies, replacement candidates, design-timeframe/regime deployment, larger
  universes, and seven-to-nine-year histories produced READY=0.
- Real OI coverage was only about 30 days and the named OI strategy did not consume OI; a valid
  long-history OI hypothesis therefore remains data-blocked, not validated.
- Order flow/CVD had approximately zero gross directional edge and was largely OHLCV-redundant.
- Perp/spot basis and Binance-to-MEXC catch-up contained information, but the effect was below
  spread and cost and requires a fee/latency envelope Lynxion does not possess.
- Historical L2 depth and liquidations were not integrated and were explicitly backtest-blocked.
- Funding×flow did not improve the unstable funding-only lead.

The controlled C-01–C-15 campaign independently reinforces, rather than overturns, that boundary.

## Sequential Decision

Under `sequential-edge-research-policy-v1.md`, another OHLCV, funding, cross-sectional, reversal,
or retail-minute microstructure variant is inadmissible. It would be a correlated repeat on opened
data, not a new independent hypothesis.

The remaining valid paths are:

1. Continue C-11 prospective severe-funding shadow collection without orders.
2. Introduce auditable historical L2/liquidation/OI data with sufficient multi-year coverage.
3. Change the execution envelope enough to make the known sub-cost cross-venue information
   capturable, then revalidate it under measured fees and latency.
4. Formally retain the no-live-trading disposition if neither data nor execution changes.

Paths 2–3 may require paid data, new services, security review, and material architecture or
execution decisions. They are operator-gated. Historical evidence alone cannot authorize live funds.
