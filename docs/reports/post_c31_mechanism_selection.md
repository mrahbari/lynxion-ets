# Post-C31 Mechanism Selection

## Verdict

Do not create another historical variant from the opened Lynxion panels. C-31 observed many large
Spot shocks but no qualifying underreaction, and its frozen response threshold cannot be relaxed.
Earlier candidates already cover the available directional and convergence families closely enough
that another threshold or feature combination would increase false-discovery risk.

## Admitted research direction

The next direction is a prospective, market-neutral cross-venue perpetual dislocation study. The
hypothesis is that a sufficiently large *executable* price disagreement between two perpetual
venues converges after paying both legs' fees, spreads, funding, latency, and slippage. This differs
from prior Spot/perpetual basis work because both legs are perpetual contracts on separate venues
and the signal is a simultaneously observable executable spread rather than a directional forecast.

The immediate work is not outcome evaluation. It is a frozen, read-only data-feasibility contract
and forward collector. A prospective boundary must exist before conditional observations are
recorded. Historical backfill cannot enter the cohort, and unavailable comparable public data is a
fail-closed data result.

## Safety and interpretation

This admission authorizes public market-data research only. It does not authorize authentication,
orders, account changes, production integration, or a profitability claim. Even a historical or
prospective statistical pass would still require separate execution-realism and operator gates.
