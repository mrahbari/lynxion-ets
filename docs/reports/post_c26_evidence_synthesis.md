# Post-C26 Evidence Synthesis

## Decision

C-26 rejects causal BTC-lead/alt-underreaction in both primary and reverse periods. Its small
positive SHORT and ETH/SOL cells are post-outcome diagnostics and cannot be promoted. A directional
response-band, shock-threshold, horizon, or symbol variant is not admissible on the opened panel.

After 26 historical candidates and multiple family closures, another transformation of the same
15-minute OHLC/funding/OI/depth/taker-ratio panels is not justified. The next safe step is **new-data
feasibility**, not another candidate.

## New Evidence Source

Official Binance Futures aggregate-trade archives expose individual aggregate trade size, price,
timestamp, and maker-side classification. They can distinguish trade-size concentration and
large-aggressor bursts from C-21/C-22's candle-level taker quote ratio. A future candidate could be
admitted only if a validated point-in-time panel supports a clearly specified large-trade mechanism.

## Frozen Feasibility Boundary

- Census official BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT aggregate-trade archives and checksum companions for
  2023-01-01 through 2026-08-29.
- Record archive count, compressed bytes, date coverage, missing dates, and projected local working
  storage before downloading bulk data.
- Feasibility work may fetch listings and a minimal schema sample only; it may not build signals,
  count conditional outcomes, download the full corpus, or preregister a candidate.
- If storage or transfer requirements are disproportionate, stop with a documented NO-GO rather
  than consuming the remaining workspace capacity.
- No production, broker, risk, trailing, symbol-admission, paper-order, or real-order action.

## Next Unit

Implement and run a read-only archive census with an explicit storage gate. Candidate admission is
forbidden until the source is feasible and its schema/integrity can be validated reproducibly.
