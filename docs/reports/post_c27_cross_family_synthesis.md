# Post-C27 Cross-Family Synthesis

## Decision

Pause historical candidate creation. No currently available, unopened mechanism clears the
sequential admission gate without reusing a rejected family, reslicing an opened outcome, or adding
an ungrounded threshold/horizon variant.

## Evidence

- C-27's concentrated-aggressor reversal is negative before as well as after costs, across every
  primary fold, both sides, both years, and the earlier temporal period. Concentration, imbalance,
  direction, and horizon variants are therefore not admitted on the opened panel.
- Taker-flow continuation already failed two independent universes (C-21/C-22). Order-book
  continuation and liquidity withdrawal failed C-18/C-19. OI continuation/reversal failed
  C-16/C-17. Direct carry and basis convergence failed C-23 through C-25. BTC-lead propagation
  failed C-26. Recombining these inputs is not a distinct causal mechanism.
- C-10/C-12's strongest historical funding evidence is already isolated in the prospective C-11
  cohort. Historical threshold tuning would contaminate that confirmatory path.
- TASK-0129 found actionable execution-risk evidence—profit giveback and an unresolved 10x exchange
  versus 5x local leverage inconsistency—but not a valid exit-policy outcome. These require
  authoritative observability and forward/OOS evidence, not retrospective threshold selection.

## Next Admitted Work

1. Perform a bounded, read-only leverage-consistency audit from configuration through broker
   admission and authoritative exchange state. Prove whether the path fails closed; do not change
   leverage or production settings in the audit.
2. Specify the minimum event ledger needed to correlate manager evaluations, stop replacements,
   broker visibility, trigger basis, fills, restart hydration, and realized giveback.
3. Preregister any profit-lock comparison only on a new forward boundary after that observability
   exists. Continue C-11 independently.

The official aggregate-trade panel remains a reproducible research asset, but its opened C-27
outcome does not authorize more historical variants.
