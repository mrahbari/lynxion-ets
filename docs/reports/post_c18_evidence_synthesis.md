# Post-C18 Evidence Synthesis

## Closed Evidence

- Static near-book imbalance continuation is negative in every primary fold, side, and symbol and
  in the 2023 temporal sample.
- Reversing C-18 on the same opened outcomes would be post-result direction selection and is
  prohibited.
- OI expansion/continuation and contraction/reversal are both independently closed.
- Funding rebound remains prospective-only under C-11 and cannot be promoted before its frozen
  gates complete.

## Next Distinct Mechanism

The retained book panel supports a dynamic liquidity-withdrawal question that C-18 did not test:
whether a four-hour fall in ask-side depth relative to bid-side depth precedes upward price motion,
and the symmetric bid-withdrawal condition precedes downward motion. This uses changes in liquidity
provision rather than the static sign of depth imbalance.

Only one specification is admitted as C-19. Static imbalance direction, outcome-derived reversal,
alternative horizons, deeper-level variants, and post-result symbol/regime filters are excluded.
