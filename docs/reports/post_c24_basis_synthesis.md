# Post-C24 Basis-Convergence Synthesis

## Decision

C-24 is rejected because only seven primary and two reverse pairs survived its frozen economic
floor. Its positive net outcome is a sparse diagnostic result, not an edge claim. The opened
six-symbol panel may not be re-run with a lower basis floor, lower percentile, longer period,
different exit, or selected symbols.

One independent historical confirmation is admissible under the sequential policy using the
unchanged C-24 mechanics on a disjoint universe whose spot/perpetual basis-conditioned outcomes
remain unopened: DOGEUSDT, LINKUSDT, LTCUSDT, DOTUSDT, and AVAXUSDT.

## Why One Confirmation Is Justified

- The direct basis-convergence family has been opened only once, not falsified twice.
- C-24's gross movement cleared primary costs, so the mechanism is economically capable in the
  observed rare events even though robustness gates failed.
- The new universe is disjoint and cannot increase the C-24 sample retrospectively.
- Signal, direction, 0.40% floor, causal p99/2,880 lookback, convergence level, timeout, funding,
  cost, and execution rules must remain unchanged.

## Boundary

The next unit is data acquisition only: obtain and integrity-check official native 15-minute spot
archives for the five disjoint symbols and align them to the already acquired C-22 futures panel.
No C-25 outcome or conditional event count may be opened during acquisition. C-25 must then be
preregistered before evaluation. If the unchanged independent confirmation fails, the direct
basis-convergence family closes.
