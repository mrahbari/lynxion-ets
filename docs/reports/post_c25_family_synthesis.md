# Post-C25 Family Synthesis

## Closed Family

C-24 and the unchanged disjoint C-25 confirmation both found economically large but extremely rare
positive basis-convergence events. Neither approached its frozen sample/breadth requirements; C-25
had only two primary events and no reverse events. Direct basis convergence is therefore closed.
No basis threshold, percentile, holding interval, convergence level, universe slice, or cost variant
is admissible on either opened panel.

## Next Admissible Mechanism

The next candidate family is **causal cross-market lead/lag underreaction**: a completed BTC shock
is the market-context event, and an altcoin is eligible only when its contemporaneous completed
move materially underreacts. Entry would occur at the next exact common open in the BTC direction.

This is distinct from prior work:

- C-05 ranked simultaneous cross-sectional momentum; it did not test BTC as a causal leader.
- C-06 tested market-neutral extreme reversal, not delayed propagation.
- C-07 tested each symbol's own price/volume acceleration.
- C-14/C-15 tested long-horizon own-series momentum.
- C-21/C-22 tested own-symbol aggressive taker-flow continuation.

The mechanism uses already validated native futures bars and needs no new paid or execution data.
Its outcome membership has not been computed. A candidate is admissible only after freezing the BTC
shock definition, underreaction ratio, direction, universe, exact timing, overlap, holding period,
funding, costs, folds, reverse period, clustered uncertainty, breadth, and concentration gates.

## Guardrails

- No reuse of C-24/C-25 positive events or any previously reported diagnostic cell.
- No production, risk, trailing, symbol-admission, paper-order, or real-order action.
