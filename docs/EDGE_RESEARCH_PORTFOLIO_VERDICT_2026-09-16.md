# Edge Research Portfolio Verdict — 2026-09-16

## Verdict

`PROMISING_INSUFFICIENT_FORWARD_EVIDENCE`

No statistically defensible edge has been identified. The only surviving directional mechanism is
the already-frozen C-11 severe-negative-funding rebound, whose current positive descriptive result
is far below its prospective evidence gate. C-35 and C-33 remain valid background experiments but
have not produced decision-grade economics.

## What we learned

1. **15-minute reversal plus aggressive flow:** rejected. Conditioning changed net expectancy by
   only +0.0039 percentage points; net results, every fold, every symbol, and both sides remained
   negative.
2. **Funding state:** broad level/sign/change variants and flow interactions are unstable or
   non-incremental. Only the severe-tail rebound remains causally plausible, and C-11 already owns
   its unopened forward boundary.
3. **Own-series momentum:** no distinct low-cost variant survived C-14/C-15. Volatility scaling is
   sizing, not new directional information.
4. **Liquidation/OI:** continuation and exhaustion were both rejected on historical OI. C-33's
   censored forward feed is valid but too slow for the near-term discovery clock, and it has no
   causal overlap with the historical OI panel.
5. **Order-flow toxicity:** the existing taker-flow representation is weak and OHLCV-redundant.
   Direction, reversal, concentration, liquidity-state, cross-venue, and mean-reversion-conditioning
   roles all failed.

## Strongest surviving hypothesis

C-11 severe-negative-funding rebound is strongest only in a relative sense. It currently has four
completed outcomes across BTC and ETH, +0.2524% funding-inclusive descriptive expectancy, and PF
1.2693. Three of four outcomes are ETH and the sample is too small for independence, temporal
breadth, confidence, or concentration claims. Its frozen gate requires at least 100 completed
outcomes, at least 30 per symbol, positive clustered confidence, both symbols positive, three
positive quartiles, PF above one, and concentration control. Verdict therefore remains
`COLLECTING`; this is not promotable edge evidence.

## Strongest falsification evidence

The strongest new falsification is the A1 reversal-plus-flow result because it used a large causal
panel, realistic 0.30%/0.50% costs, four chronological folds, six symbols, both sides, and clustered
uncertainty. The wholly negative result and negligible incremental change directly reject the
suggested rescue mechanism rather than merely failing for low sample size.

## Current bottleneck

The primary bottleneck is **information**, followed by **economic friction**:

- The common public historical panels—OHLCV, funding, OI, taker flow, Spot/perpetual flow, and depth
  proxies—have already supported multiple independent negative mechanisms.
- Recombining or retuning those opened panels now has more data-snooping risk than information gain.
- Small gross effects are generally below realistic round-trip costs.
- The distinct forward information sets (severe funding and censored forced liquidations) are valid
  but sparse and calendar-time limited.

This is not currently an architecture bottleneck. More services, models, L2 infrastructure, or
production strategy code would not repair the missing evidence.

## Engineering spent versus information gained

The bounded cycle changed no production strategy, risk, broker, or execution file. It used one
disposable exploratory script and one focused test module; the other decisions reused existing
artifacts. Repository evidence across the cycle touched 24 tracked files, mostly append-only cohort
data, task records, reports, and the decision board. The information return was high relative to
engineering cost: four new implementation directions were stopped, one forward hypothesis was
preserved, and no permanent research platform was added.

## Decision and next operating state

- Continue C-11, C-35, and C-33 unchanged under their frozen gates.
- Do not build another strategy family from the current historical panels.
- Do not promote, retune, or implement any edge claim from this cycle.
- Continue minimal verified correctness/risk/execution work independently because it protects the
  system without claiming profitability.
- Admit another edge family only if it brings a distinct causal mechanism and either a genuinely
  new point-in-time information set or a clean unopened prospective boundary with a practical
  decision horizon.

The next forced portfolio decision occurs on a frozen cohort gate transition, a material new
information source, or a genuinely distinct operator/Codex hypothesis—not merely another parameter
choice.
