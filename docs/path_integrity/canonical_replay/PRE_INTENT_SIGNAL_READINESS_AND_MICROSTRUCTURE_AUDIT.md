# Pre-intent signal readiness and microstructure contract audit

Starting HEAD `3c16b9e`. Read-only source/archive inspection plus a six-bar, two-symbol deterministic fixture. **No forward returns, PnL or baseline were calculated.** Research object/preregistration: `PRE_INTENT_SIGNAL_STUDY_PREREGISTRATION.md`.

## A — deepest causal boundary reached

The closed-bar driver passes validated completed OHLCV to the existing `HistoricalCandleWatcherAdapter`, then through the actual routed `SignalProcessor._process_observation`, `EngineService.process_observation`, `SignalProcessor._process_interpreted_signal`, and `FusionService.fuse_signals([signal])`. The resulting routed `FusedSignal` is the deepest **pre-intent** output exercised without invoking `DecisionPipeline`'s synthetic microstructure fallbacks. Its `dominant_bias`, `direction`, `confidence`, event timestamp and symbol are the research variables; neither confidence nor direction is a StrategyManager decision. Fusion currently receives a one-element interpreted-signal list in this route. This is a **defined candle-only research subset**, not all Live watcher/Fusion semantics.

|---|---|---|
| Completed bar OHLCV, symbol, timeframe, close time | ARCHIVED_DIRECT *conditional on archive timestamp provenance* | `ClosedBarDecisionDriver` supplies completed bars; `HistoricalClosedBarSource` validates ordering/gaps/geometry. The normalized routed-event test `test_pre_intent_engine_fusion_two_symbol_prefix_and_fresh_scope` passes repeated runs, fresh scopes and prefix-versus-future on six bars per symbol. It proves only this fixture-level `MarketObservation` → `InterpretedSignal` → `FusedSignal` prefix. It does not prove the historical CSV's timestamp semantics, sufficient statistical power, or a complete archive-level prefix test. `PRE_INTENT_EDGE_MEASUREMENT_NOT_READY`: the smallest immediate blocker is qualified, continuous, point-in-time historical candle provenance/close-time mapping for a preregistered cohort (including sufficient common coverage), followed by the same prefix test on that qualified archive. ## B — full Strategy/ExecutionIntent data contract

The enabled adapters pass their `data_buffer[-1]` to `DecisionPipeline.process_execution_intent`. `BaseStrategyAdapter.ensure_data_buffer` may load historical bars through the Live broker registry when no explicit loader is bound; replay's explicit loader is fail-closed. The one-symbol causal feed currently supplies only actual OHLCV and timestamp. `latest_bar.get()` treats *missing keys* as defaults; present-but-`None`/stale/nonfinite values are not freshness-validated here. No source-level merge from `LiquidityWatcher`'s separate book observation into the strategy bar was found in this bounded path. This does **not** prove how often Live takes a fallback: that requires runtime traces.

### Fallback forensics

When `obi_ratio` is absent, BUY receives `+0.15`, SELL `-0.15`; absent `cvd` receives `+1.0`/`-1.0`. These **always satisfy** the current 0.1 OBI and zero-sign CVD confirmation for their respective sides; missing data can therefore change a would-be rejection into confirmation and can change existence of BUY/SELL intent. When either BBO field is absent, it becomes `current_price ± 0.0001 * current_price`; with both absent, modeled spread is 0.0002 (< optimizer's 0.001 maximum), and maker limit price is synthetic. This can change admission and entry price. Pipeline confidence is separately resolved from fused metadata or `calculate_dynamic_metrics`; these fallbacks do not directly assign confidence, but may change which intent/confidence exists. No frequency claim for Live or previous experiments: historical strategy-row backtester provenance is `LEGACY_NONCANONICAL`, but use of this particular fallback per past conclusion is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` without event/input logs. Do not retroactively recast frozen research.

### SetupEngine time

`SetupEngine.scan_for_setups` returns early with <5 prices, then creates `ExchangeTimestamp(int(datetime.now().timestamp()*1000))` for candidate setups. The timestamp enters `CandidateSetup`; adapter `_is_setup_fresh` first compares trigger price to latest close and may pass without timestamp, otherwise compares setup/bar timestamps within five seconds. The wall-clock value can therefore alter admission on a reached path, even though it does not alter sweep/ATR/VWAP formula directly. Replacing it with event time is a narrow clock seam in principle, but its effective Live fallback/freshness behavior and all adapters have not been characterized enough to prove no economic-semantic change. No source change is made in this audit.

Full Strategy/ExecutionIntent edge gate remains **BLOCKED** by direct microstructure provenance, setup event-time semantics, multi-symbol adapter history isolation and full decision-level prefix/parity. No economic baseline is authorized.
