# Funding-State Feasibility Review — 2026-09-16

## Question

Does existing evidence justify a new minimal test of funding as an ex-ante market-state modifier,
distinct from rejected carry/basis and the already-open severe-negative-funding rebound family?

## Existing evidence

- Phase-14 found near-zero broad linear/rank prediction and no stable information in positive level,
  expansion, contraction, or sign transition. Only extreme-negative funding followed by a BTC/ETH
  24–72h rebound survived directionally; SOL contradicted it.
- C-10 repaired the lookahead/cost problem for BTC/ETH historical holdout and generated the severe
  tail lead that is already frozen prospectively as C-11.
- C-12 extended the same causal p10 rebound to BNB/XRP/ADA. It returned positive +0.5403% expectancy,
  PF 1.2763, four positive folds, and three positive symbols, but its frozen bootstrap interval
  [-0.0549%, +1.1266%] crossed zero and the candidate was correctly rejected.
- Phase-19 found that aggressive-flow confirmation did not reliably improve funding-only: it helped
  BTC but materially hurt ETH and SOL. The positive cells were inherited from funding.
- Carry/basis economics were separately rejected. Funding persistence predicts funding, not price.
- C-11 is the only unopened forward severe-tail test and must remain sealed until its frozen gates.

## Mechanism review

- Mechanism: severe negative funding can represent short crowding and forced/deleveraging pressure
  that later mean-reverts.
- Counterparty: late crowded shorts or forced sellers entering after displacement.
- Persistence: episodic crowding can recur, but competition and costs should remove broad/simple
  versions; only severe tails remain plausible.
- Failure: symbol-specific market structure, persistent bearish information, correlated market
  shocks, and slow sparse events.
- Execution: existing data supports it, but the valid prospective rate is too slow for the primary
  investor horizon.

## Decision

`NO NEW FUNDING-STATE TEST — CONTINUE FROZEN C-11 ONLY`.

Broad state variants are already falsified or unstable. Flow conditioning is non-incremental. A new
level/sign/percentile/acceleration/persistence variant would be parameter recycling around opened
evidence. The severe-tail mechanism already has the correct independent prospective test in C-11.

Do not inspect C-11 aggregate economics early, relax severity, widen symbols, change horizon, or
create a new funding candidate. Continue C-11 at low cost and move the primary research clock to the
next distinct family.
