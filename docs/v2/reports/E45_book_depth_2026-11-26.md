# E45 — Order-book depth: imbalance direction and thin-book risk (2026-11-26)

**Data.** New information: free Binance `bookDepth` archive.
- 40 most liquid perpetuals, 2023-01..2026-11.
- 37,167 daily files, reduced to 5-minute buckets of order-book notional within ±0.2–5 % of price.
- Stored in `data/lake/binance_um/bookdepth_5m`, 836 MB.

**Method.** Pre-registered in commit dd71097, before the data was read.
- Script: `research/v2/experiments/e45_book_depth.py`.
- DEV 2023–24; TEST 2025–26, read once.

## H1 — does order-book imbalance predict the next 4 hours?

**Signal.** Imbalance = (bid − ask) notional within ±1 %, as a 7-day z-score. Trade in its direction when |z| ≥ 2; hold 4h.

| Window | Events | Net bp/trade | Gross bp/trade | t (day) | Symbols + |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEV | 20,609 | −12.98 | +1.0 | −6.7 | 8 % |
| TEST | 22,096 | −12.23 | +1.8 | −4.7 | 13 % |

**FAIL.**
- Gross is about +1–2 bp: the imbalance carries no usable information over 4 hours.
- The strongly negative net is just the 14 bp round-trip cost.
- By side, the result follows the market's drift and flips between windows: DEV long +12.9 / short −11.2 bp gross; TEST long −5.1 / short +8.2 bp gross.

## H2 — is a thin book riskier?

**Signal.** Thin = total notional within ±1 % below 30 % of its 7-day median (about 0.6 % of hours).

| Window | Thin hours | Next-4h range vs normal | Symbols with a higher range |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEV | 223 | 1.32× | 92 % |
| TEST | 222 | 1.19× | 76 % |

**FAIL against the pre-registered bar (≥ 1.5×).**
- The effect is real and consistent across symbols, but small: 19–32 % more range for an event that occurs in under 1 % of hours.
- As an entry filter it would change little.

## Decision

- Nothing adopted. No change to the platform, the Radar or XS-COMBO.
- The depth lake stays for future pre-registered questions, e.g. execution sizing on real capital.
