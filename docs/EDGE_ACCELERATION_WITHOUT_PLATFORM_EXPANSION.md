# Lynxion — Edge Acceleration Without Platform Expansion

**Adopted:** 2026-09-14

**Status:** ACTIVE SUPPLEMENTAL DIRECTIVE

**Purpose:** Shorten the path to an economic answer while preventing endless research-platform,
strategy, candidate, and infrastructure expansion.

This directive supplements `tasks/00-right-path.md` and the frozen candidate protocols. It does not
restart completed work and cannot weaken an already-opened preregistration.

## Core rule

For the next research cycle:

`ANALYZE FIRST — DO NOT BUILD FIRST`

New code is allowed only when required to answer one specific falsifiable question. It must be
minimal, isolated, reviewable, and disposable where practical. Existing collectors, bounded
integrity verification, and verified P0 correctness repairs are not prohibited by this rule.

## 1. Existing frozen candidates

Continue C-35, C-11, and C-33 under their existing frozen protocols. Do not retune, add features,
change thresholds, import history, or alter gates after outcomes are visible.

Operational reporting must include:

- eligible-event rate;
- completed-outcome rate;
- effective independent-event rate using a disclosed cluster unit;
- symbol, time, and side breadth;
- estimated calendar time to the next decision checkpoint and frozen minimum.

An operational label such as `TOO SLOW — DEPRIORITIZED AS PRIMARY PATH` does not overwrite the
candidate's scientific verdict. Low-cost collection may continue in the background. Any breadth
expansion is a new version/candidate with a new boundary and preregistration; it cannot modify the
opened cohort.

## 2. No production strategy or platform expansion

Do not implement funding carry, basis trading, full L2 capture, ML, HMM/GMM, a new execution
architecture, permanent feature infrastructure, or paid-data integration in this decision phase.

This restriction does not delay a verified correctness fix for risk bypass, duplicate/unbounded
entry, protection, broker acknowledgement, restart hydration, data integrity, or lookahead.

## 3. Funding and basis feasibility

Use existing accessible evidence before acquiring or building anything. Evaluate usable spot/perp
pairs and venues, actual funding schedules/rates, persistence, concentration, committed capital,
return on committed capital, basis, liquidation, borrow, transfer, venue, and two-leg operational
risk. Determine whether the current architecture can safely manage both legs without major work.

Use the signed identity:

```text
NET HEDGED RETURN =
funding cashflow
+ spot-leg PnL
+ perpetual-leg PnL
- fees
- spread
- slippage
- borrow cost
- hedge-leg funding
- transfer and operational friction
```

Do not assume settlements are independent. Verdict:

- `PROMISING — SMALL IMPLEMENTATION JUSTIFIED`
- `REJECT — ECONOMICS INSUFFICIENT`
- `INSUFFICIENT DATA — COLLECT MINIMAL DATA ONLY`

No strategy is built unless the first verdict is supported.

## 4. L2 value before infrastructure

Reuse C-18/C-19, Phase-17, C-32, and existing public archive/API findings before considering new
collection. Determine what is already captured, what free APIs expose, what is missing, the exact
causal hypothesis, expected incremental information, storage/engineering cost, and whether a small
temporary sample can answer the question.

Do not build permanent storage, book reconstruction, feature services, or production integration in
this phase. Verdict:

- `HIGH INFORMATION VALUE — MINIMAL COLLECTION JUSTIFIED`
- `LOW INFORMATION VALUE — DO NOT BUILD`
- `UNKNOWN — RUN SMALL TEMPORARY SAMPLE FIRST`

## 5. Historical loss decomposition

Do not reopen historical optimization. Estimate only reproducible lower bounds or overlapping
contributions from sizing/risk, execution, strategy economics, fees, exits, and symbol/side
concentration. Preserve an `UNKNOWN` residual. Do not force overlapping causes to sum to 100% and do
not filter post-hoc to manufacture edge.

The only objective is to determine whether a rejected family was invalidly condemned by a verified
execution defect. Reopening a family requires a distinct causal reason and an unopened boundary.

## 6. Futility and calendar budget

No cohort remains the primary research path indefinitely. For each active cohort define an
operational review date, effective independent-event unit, breadth requirement, projected time to
the frozen gate, and a futility/deprioritization rule.

These operational decisions do not rewrite scientific gates. A cheap slow cohort may continue in
the background, but investor delivery and the bounded research cycle do not wait for it.

## 7. Hypothesis budget

The active autonomous edge-discovery cycle permits **up to five**, not exactly five, primary
economically independent mechanism families under `docs/AUTONOMOUS_EDGE_DISCOVERY_MISSION.md`.
Zero is acceptable if feasibility gates fail. Parameter variants and conditioning modifiers do not
count as independent families. A stronger sixth lead must replace or park a weaker active family.

Do not generate sequential candidates merely to show activity. At the end of the bounded cycle,
force a portfolio-level decision: promote, reject, collect a specifically missing minimal input, or
declare the current information set insufficient.

## 8. Engineering budget

Before implementation, estimate files, LOC, persistent data, services/processes, operational
complexity, regression surface, time to answer, and expected information value. Prefer scripts or
research modules touching few files, no production runtime, no new service, and removable outputs.

If major architecture is required before economic plausibility can be demonstrated, reject or defer
the idea.

## 9. Decision gate

Before new research implementation, produce a ranked table covering C-35, C-11, C-33,
funding/basis, L2/microstructure, and at most one strongly justified independent mechanism.

Allowed decisions:

- `CONTINUE`
- `MINIMAL TEST`
- `DEFER`
- `REJECT`

The artifact must identify what should not be built, the single highest-value next task, its
engineering budget, and the measurable KEEP/REJECT outcome. Then automatically proceed only with
the highest-ranked safe `MINIMAL TEST`. Existing frozen collection and correctness work continue.

## Final principle

The goal is not to build a better research platform. The goal is to discover whether Lynxion has a
usable edge. Every new line of code must earn its existence by shortening the path to that answer.
