# Time-Series Momentum Feasibility Review — 2026-09-16

## Question

Does a causally distinct, lower-turnover, volatility-normalized own-series momentum mechanism remain
after the frozen C-14/C-15 evidence, without reopening cross-sectional momentum or manufacturing a
threshold variant?

## Existing evidence

- C-14 already tested the canonical low-turnover own-series mechanism: sign of trailing 180-day
  return, monthly decisions, 28-day hold, six liquid perpetuals, next-open execution, 0.30% primary
  and 0.50% stress costs, four chronological folds, reverse-time sample, and month-cluster bootstrap.
- C-14 primary N=228 returned -2.5269% expectancy, PF 0.7286, one positive fold, three positive
  symbols, and a confidence interval crossing zero. The earlier reverse period was strongly positive
  but failed to reproduce later.
- C-14's LONG-only clue was given one independent disjoint-universe confirmation in C-15 without
  changing the signal. C-15 was superficially positive (+1.4752%, PF 1.1504, N=82) but failed
  clustered confidence, fold breadth, symbol breadth, and concentration gates.
- Cross-sectional momentum, short-horizon breakout/acceleration, and taker-flow continuation were
  separately rejected.

## Volatility-normalization assessment

Volatility scaling changes position weights and risk contribution; it does not create directional
information when the underlying unit-return signal is negative and temporally unstable. Applying it
post-result to C-14/C-15 would be a sizing reinterpretation of opened outcomes, not a new economic
mechanism. A volatility threshold or alternative lookback/hold would likewise be parameter recycling
unless a new causal state and unopened boundary were established first.

Funding omission in C-14 cannot plausibly rescue -2.5269% average primary return; adding funding to
the same opened signal would not supply an independent confirmation boundary.

## Decision

`REJECT — NO DISTINCT LOW-COST MOMENTUM TEST JUSTIFIED`.

Do not retune C-14/C-15, apply post-result volatility filters, or create another momentum candidate.
Reconsider only if a genuinely different mechanism is identified from new unopened information,
not from lookback, threshold, side, or sizing changes.
