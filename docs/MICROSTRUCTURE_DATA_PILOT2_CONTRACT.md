## Raw provenance and causal reconstruction

- Each raw source WebSocket message byte string and raw REST snapshot response byte string is preserved exactly as a base64 payload within an append-only gzip JSONL envelope. Envelope fields are `kind`, `symbol`, unique session ID, local `receipt_utc_ns` from `time.time_ns()`, distinct `receipt_monotonic_ns` from `time.monotonic_ns()`, and `payload_b64`; snapshot envelopes additionally store request/response UTC nanoseconds. JSONL file order is local receive order within a session; source `E`, `T`, `U`, `u`, `pu`, and snapshot `lastUpdateId` remain in unmodified decoded payload bytes. UTC wall receipt is not exchange event time, and monotonic time is not UTC. Absolute NTP offset is `UNKNOWN` unless independent evidence is recorded. Collector-originated errors are labeled separately from observed market data.
- Finalized raw chunks are exclusive-create, never reopened or overwritten, with SHA-256, byte count, record count and timestamp/identifier bounds in an atomically replaced manifest. Raw chunks interrupted before gzip finalization remain **orphan evidence**: record path, bytes and SHA-256 at restart, do not silently repair, promote to finalized coverage, or delete. Manifest and supervision state are durable separate metadata; end-of-pilot independent hash readback is mandatory.
- For each symbol/epoch, buffer depth updates while obtaining a fresh REST snapshot, discard pre-snapshot updates with `u < lastUpdateId`, require a first retained bridge `U <= lastUpdateId <= u`, then require `pu == previous u`. Absolute price-level quantities update bids/asks; zero deletes a level. Finite positive prices, nonnegative quantities and a nonempty uncrossed book are required. A derived book is valid only after the bridge. Raw exchange payloads are **OBSERVED**; reconstructed quotes/state are **DERIVED_FROM_OBSERVED**. Neither proxy, synthetic/default OBI/CVD, nor a trading signal is produced.
- On unresolvable sequence mismatch, conflicting duplicate/out-of-order event, invalid book, parse/schema/clock fault, transport loss, stale stream, process death, or raw-write failure: invalidate book; record error/gap; begin a distinct epoch with a new valid snapshot/bridge before publishing derived state. Exact duplicates may be counted/ignored according to source continuity. Never interpolate unknown intermediate states or infer them from candles, aggregated depth, or later snapshots. A missing interval remains missing even if later sessions are clean.
- Reject a depth message with local receipt `>=` frozen stop before raw append. Completed metadata/finalization may occur after stop but cannot turn post-stop depth into formal data.

## Process, clock, disk and monitoring policy

- A macOS LaunchAgent starts the committed bounded supervisor without manual foreground watching, wrapped in `caffeinate -is` for idle/system-sleep prevention while it runs. It does **not** prove survival of host shutdown or forced closed-lid sleep. The supervisor checks child liveness and per-symbol heartbeat about every two seconds; after the startup grace, age above 25 seconds is stale. Unexpected child death or stale feed is recorded with PID/reason/time, followed by a fresh process/epoch/snapshot. More than three child deaths in ten minutes is durable `TERMINAL_FAILURE`, not an infinite loop. A failure of the supervisor itself is exposed by LaunchAgent state and periodic independent heartbeat monitoring.
- Preserve local wall and monotonic receipt clocks distinctly. A wall-minus-monotonic discontinuity above one second fails closed and is reported as `wall_monotonic_discontinuity`; it is **not** labeled an NTP jump without independent evidence. Preserve source `E/T` exactly. No latency-alpha analysis is authorized.
- Preflight requires both public feeds and snapshots demonstrated by the segregated readiness soak, a clean LaunchAgent setup, and at least **25 GiB free disk** before activation: approximately three times Pilot-1's 4.85 GB seven-day finalized raw volume plus the collector's fixed 10 GiB reserve, rounded conservatively. The collector checks at start/per session and every 1,000 events; crossing below 10 GiB is an explicit failure, not a reason to delete prior data or continue treating the feed as healthy. Continue monitoring disk reserve, heartbeat, finalized hashes, orphan count, sessions, snapshots, gaps, duplicates, reconnects, wall/monotonic discontinuities and write errors. No DNS polling beyond normal bounded recovery; no alternate source.

## Frozen measurement verdict and report

At the exact stop, no extension is permitted. Evaluate per-symbol valid coverage as the union of each **finalized, valid session's** recorded first-to-last applied-event UTC **processing** interval, clipped to the formal boundary. This is an **approximation of reconstructed valid-session span**, not proof that every millisecond between updates was observed. Raw receipt timestamps remain separately preserved and must not be relabeled as processing time. Interrupted/orphan sessions contribute zero valid span unless already finalized, independently verifiable segments exist; unknown state across epochs never counts. Also report actual unobserved intervals and the longest gap, sequence faults, host/process/DNS incidents, any possible silent-feed uncertainty, and that zero in-session gaps is not seven-day continuity.

- `MICROSTRUCTURE_COLLECTION_PILOT_PASS` only if both symbols have at least **99%** approximate valid-session coverage over the frozen seven days, neither has an unobserved interval over **60 seconds**, all reported valid sessions replay with valid snapshot/bridge and no unresolved sequence error, finalized-file hashes/record counts match **100%**, raw provenance/timestamp semantics are intact, and the start/stop boundary was respected. Detected faults followed by explicit invalidation/rebootstrap may occur, but their missing time still counts against coverage/gap rules.

Clearly quantify missing/unobserved data; do not call it continuous. A partial result is not permission to extend the pilot.
- `MICROSTRUCTURE_COLLECTION_PILOT_FAIL` if source/provenance or timestamp semantics are invalid, finalized raw/manifest corruption prevents reliable audit, the boundary was not respected in a way that cannot be isolated, either symbol has less than **50%** valid-session coverage, or evidence is otherwise too incomplete for bounded qualification.
