## Major remaining uncertainties and information value

1. **Does truly new, point-in-time market information add to candles?** Existing taker-flow is mostly OHLCV-redundant and cost-negative, but full event-time BBO/L2/liquidation or cross-venue state has not been qualified at Lynxion decision instants. A clean negative incremental test would strongly shift attention away from expensive data infrastructure; a positive predictive test would still need executable economics. New feed provenance, gap/reconnect audit and a genuinely unopened boundary are the main burdens. Do not treat synthetic OBI/CVD/BBO as this evidence.
2. **Is selective opportunity choice reproducible and independent of raw direction?** The old candle-reversal Fusion path was not directional; V2's existing structural proxy had insufficient controls; Phase 3A/F4's external selection clue is underpowered and lacks a trustworthy incoming feed. Whether *observed* selection has incremental information remains unknown. Failure on a complete prospective cohort would be informative, but another OHLCV proxy conjunction on opened data would not.
3. **Does any supported predictive effect clear actual fills/costs?** Lead B directly shows an association can die at 0.30% friction. V1 gives no positive prediction and V2 no effect, so their execution simulator would not currently answer a favorable-signal question. Independent measurement of spread/slippage/funding/latency can calibrate a feasibility ceiling for future signals, but cannot by itself establish an Edge or rescue V1/V2. It needs authenticated execution only if later measurement design truly requires it; none is authorized here.

## Kill/pivot assessment and execution gap

**Evidence favors a pivot in *information*, not abandonment of Lynxion.** Multiple independent, cost-aware OHLCV/flow/OI/funding variants did not produce a robust net edge, the Phase-6 price-only search had zero full promotions, V1 failed its narrow predictive-support criterion, and V2 could not identify its incremental contrast. This supports **low expected incremental scientific value from another post-hoc arrangement of the same opened OHLCV indicators**. It does **not** prove all causal OHLCV rules lack information: V1 was one Engine boundary, V2 never opened labels, Phase-6 had a provisional clue, and frozen prospective C-11/F4 questions remain unresolved. “Same information/new rule” requires a demonstrably independent pre-outcome mechanism and unopened boundary; simply recombining existing price features is not justified by the present record. “New information/new test” has higher potential information value but only with actual data and prospective provenance, not synthetic fallbacks.

Execution/cost measurement is useful **as a cross-cutting feasibility bound** (what effect magnitude clears fee/spread/slippage/funding), especially after Lead B's rejection. It cannot turn unsupported prediction into Edge. No production simulator, broker experiment, VST action or new cost study is authorized by this review.
