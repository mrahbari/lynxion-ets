# Lynxion Autonomous Edge Discovery Mission

**Adopted:** 2026-09-16

**Status:** ACTIVE SUPPLEMENTAL DIRECTIVE

## Mission

Discover, falsify, validate, and only when evidence supports it promote one or more statistically
defensible crypto trading edges with positive net expectancy after realistic execution costs.

Research freedom is broad. Production-change freedom remains narrow. This directive does not
restart completed work, weaken frozen protocols, open sealed outcomes, alter `NO_TRADE_DEFAULT`, or
relax risk, OOS, prospective, cost, and promotion gates.

## Operating model

- Maintain equal `OPERATOR` and `CODEX` hypothesis inboxes. Both contain leads, not facts.
- Use academic, official exchange, reproducible practitioner, and community sources in descending
  evidentiary order. Community claims can generate mechanisms but cannot prove profitability.
- For every hypothesis state mechanism, counterparty, persistence, regime, failure state,
  point-in-time information, executable cost hurdle, and immediate falsification condition.
- Search for conditional edge and allow `NO_TRADE`; do not assume a universal always-on strategy.
- Label exploratory work `EXPLORATORY`. Freeze every confirmatory contract before opening outcomes.
- Report nominal N and an event/time-clustered effective N where dependence is material.
- Every protocol needs a practical calendar horizon and futility/deprioritization rule.
- Maintain no more than five primary active mechanism families. Park a weaker family before adding
  a stronger sixth; parameter variants are not separate mechanisms.
- Rank by expected information gain per engineering hour. Prefer existing data, a disposable
  script, a reproducible artifact, and an explicit `KEEP/REJECT` decision.
- Do not build permanent L2, ML, options, paid-data, execution, watcher, or feature infrastructure
  before a bounded feasibility result justifies it.

## Initial mechanism families

1. 15-minute mean reversion conditioned on aggressive flow.
2. Funding as an ex-ante market state, not assumed carry edge.
3. Volatility-normalized time-series momentum with turnover and full costs.
4. Liquidation/OI cascade continuation versus forced-flow exhaustion.
5. Order-flow toxicity as a filter or state classifier, not automatically a strategy.

Round-number proximity, liquidity clusters, relative volume, volatility state, BTC regime, breadth,
session, spread/depth, and funding percentile are modifiers inside these families rather than new
candidate IDs.

## Promotion and escalation

No result is promoted for win rate, visual appeal, paper agreement, gross PnL, one symbol/period, or
parameter-search success. Existing net expectancy, PF, cost/stress, effective-sample, temporal,
symbol/side, concentration, drawdown, chronological OOS/WFO, and unopened prospective gates remain
mandatory.

After the bounded cycle issue one portfolio verdict: `EDGE_IDENTIFIED`,
`PROMISING_INSUFFICIENT_FORWARD_EVIDENCE`, `CURRENT_INFORMATION_SET_INSUFFICIENT`,
`CURRENT_STRATEGY_FAMILY_INSUFFICIENT`, `ECONOMIC_HURDLE_TOO_HIGH`, or
`NO_JUSTIFIED_NEXT_STEP`.

## Autonomous loop

`RESEARCH -> HYPOTHESIS -> MECHANISM REVIEW -> CHEAP FEASIBILITY -> REJECT/PREREGISTER -> DATA
INTEGRITY -> COST/STRESS -> OOS -> PROSPECTIVE -> KEEP/REJECT -> NEXT QUESTION`

C-35, C-11, and C-33 continue unchanged in the background. They do not block faster independent
research. Production implementation begins only after economic promotion.
