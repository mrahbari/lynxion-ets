## 1. Executive Summary

Lynxion Shadow AB is a Sweden-based quantitative trading and financial technology company established with the objective of building a sustainable, export-oriented, and regulation-aware investment operation. The company develops proprietary multi-engine algorithmic trading systems designed to operate across global liquid markets with strict risk governance and institutional standards.

The company is intentionally structured to scale gradually, beginning with proprietary trading and evolving into subscription-based services and, in later stages, managed capital solutions. From a migration and economic perspective, the project contributes to Sweden by creating high-skilled employment in quantitative finance and software engineering, developing proprietary financial technology intellectual property within Sweden, and generating long-term tax revenue through globally scalable services.

The business model emphasizes capital efficiency, transparency, and long-term stability rather than short-term speculative growth. The company follows a phased approach: initial proprietary trading with founder capital, followed by subscription-based quantitative services, and eventually performance-linked managed solutions in later phases.

Lynxion Shadow AB represents a credible, low-risk, and professionally structured business initiative aligned with Sweden's economic and migration objectives, demonstrating financial independence, regulatory awareness, and long-term commitment.

## 3. Market Opportunity & Problem Statement

### Global Market Context
Financial markets have increasingly shifted toward systematic and algorithmic trading. However, a significant gap remains between retail-accessible solutions and institutional-grade systems. The core problems identified include overreliance on single-strategy or curve-fitted systems, insufficient risk and drawdown controls, and lack of transparency and institutional reporting standards.

### Market Opportunity
Lynxion Shadow AB addresses this gap by offering a disciplined, technology-driven solution capable of producing risk-adjusted returns while maintaining operational transparency. The target market is global by design, allowing revenue generation outside Sweden while maintaining core operations and intellectual property within the country. This export-oriented structure aligns strongly with Swedish economic priorities.

### Problem Statement
- Retail traders lack institutional-grade risk management
- Many signal providers suffer from overfitting and poor drawdown control
- Hedge funds face high costs, rigid structures, and slow innovation

### Market Opportunity
- Growing demand for algorithmic and automated trading solutions
- Increasing interest in alternative investments and systematic strategies
- Global access to FX, metals, indices, and crypto markets

Lynxion Shadow addresses these gaps by combining adaptive algorithms with strict capital protection logic.

## 4. Product & Solution

### Core Offering
A proprietary quantitative trading system composed of multiple independent engines operating across multiple timeframes and instruments. The system implements the complete workflow: Watcher → Engine → Fusion → Strategy → Broker, with enterprise-grade risk management.

### Key Features
- Multi-engine strategy diversification
- Dynamic position sizing
- Volatility-aware execution
- Regime and trend filtering
- Built-in drawdown and exposure limits
- Real-time monitoring and alerts

### System Architecture
The system follows hexagonal architecture with clear separation of concerns:
- **Domain Layer**: Pure business logic with interfaces (ports)
- **Application Layer**: Orchestration and use cases (orchestrators)
- **Infrastructure Layer**: Concrete implementations (adapters)

### Output Formats
- Proprietary trading execution
- Subscription-based access (signals or managed automation)
- Institutional-ready performance reporting

## 5. Technology Architecture

### System Design
The architecture implements multiple specialized components:

- **Central risk management layer** with hard limits
- **Execution abstraction** for multi-broker support

### Watcher System

### Fusion System
- **Signal Aggregation**: Multi-signal combination
- **Weighted Fusion**: Confidence-based weighting
- **Correlation Adjustment**: Redundancy reduction
- **Adaptive Fusion**: Market regime-aware fusion

### Optimization & Research
- Parameter optimization via advanced search (Hyperopt)
- Walk-forward validation
- Robust backtesting and stress testing

### Intellectual Property
All trading logic, architecture, and optimization workflows are proprietary and retained as company IP.

## 12. Team & Organization Structure

### Dynamic Team Structure
The number of team members fluctuates as the project progresses. The typical phases and approximate staffing are:

#### 1. Research & Strategy Design (1–3 months)
- **Team:** 2–3 strategy analysts + 1 data scientist
- **Focus:** Designing strategies, analyzing data, and initial modeling. Team size is small at the start but may increase if multiple strategies are explored.

#### 2. System Development (2–4 months)
- **Team:** 2–3 algorithm developers + 1 data engineer
- **Focus:** Implementing algorithms, building data pipelines, and backend systems. Team expands during core development.

#### 3. Backtesting & Optimization (1–2 months)
- **Team:** 1–2 developers + 1 strategy analyst
- **Focus:** Historical data testing, parameter optimization, and risk simulation. Team size may shrink after initial development but grows temporarily for intensive backtesting.

#### 4. Integration (1 month)
- **Team:** 1–2 developers + 1 DevOps engineer
- **Focus:** Connecting to data streams, broker APIs, and automating orders. Small team but critical for smooth integration.

#### 5. Risk Management & Monitoring (continuous with production)
- **Team:** 1 risk specialist + 1 developer
- **Focus:** Alerts, position monitoring, and reporting. Team remains small, focusing on oversight.

#### 6. Production & Maintenance (ongoing)
- **Team:** 1–2 developers + 1 DevOps engineer
- **Focus:** Bug fixes, algorithm updates, server optimization. Team may fluctuate depending on the scale of updates or new strategies.

### Key Points:
- At the start, the team is small and mainly focused on research and strategy design.
- During core development and backtesting, the team temporarily grows.
- In production, the team size decreases, focusing on maintenance and risk control.
- For very small companies, the same people may cover multiple roles, so staffing is flexible over time.
