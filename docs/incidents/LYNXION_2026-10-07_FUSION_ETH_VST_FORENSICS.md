## 1. Prolonged neutral Fusion — P0 diagnostic task

**Facts.** The trading runner remained alive and `logs/forensic.log` continued to receive observations. In a bounded extract of 100,000 recent forensic records spanning approximately 2026-10-06 23:27 to 2026-10-07 04:41 UTC, 39,869 `FUSION` records were `NEUTRAL` with zero recorded contributors. The same extract contained 146 `BUY` and 796 `SELL` engine interpretations, so the upstream engine did not produce only neutral interpretations. The last locally journaled new VST submission was XAUTUSDT at 2026-10-06 20:20:43 UTC. Earlier `HEARTBEAT_STALE`, duplicate, queue-limit and symbol-cooldown rejections occurred, but these do not by themselves explain the entire later neutral interval.

**Verified code mismatch.** `SignalProcessor._process_interpreted_signal` calls `fusion_service.fuse_signals([signal])` for each individual signal (`infrastructure/messaging/event_system.py`). The hierarchical path expects classified direction and execution observations together (`infrastructure/fusion/hierarchical/hierarchical_fusion_service.py`). The engine emits names including `TrendMTFWatcher`, `MarketPulseWatcher`, `AnomalyMLWatcher` and `OrderFlowWSWatcher` (`infrastructure/engines/engine_service.py`), while `WatcherClassifier` keys are `trend_mtf`, `market_pulse`, `anomaly_ml` and `orderflow_ws`. A networkless read-only check returned `None` for the role of all nine emitted-style names tested. Invalid classifications are discarded. This is a reproducible platform integration defect; it can explain neutral event-bus Fusion output. The contribution of this path to every missed trade is **not established** because other opportunity paths exist. Neutral output is not proof that any rejected opportunity would have been profitable.

**Task FUSION-NEUTRAL-P0.** Trace all production opportunity paths and determine which produced the prior VST intents. Add deterministic, networkless regression characterization for name classification, batching, prefix/order isolation and end-to-end intent candidate counts. Record before/after *mechanical* parity without inspecting later returns. Do not change thresholds, fabricate missing role evidence, or send orders. ## 2. ETHUSDT SHORT VST stop — P0 execution-integrity task

**Observed account evidence.** The local trade/forward-exit records identify a SHORT entered at 2682.90, quantity 0.008, 10x leverage. At 02:01:43 the position manager observed mark 2649.02 and requested a tighter stop at 2673.50985. Its broker response was accepted at 02:02:43 after 59.75 seconds, including 11.46 seconds recorded rate-limit wait. At 02:03:43 a pending stop at 2673.51 was verified by exact exchange order ID. The manager then requested a further trailing stop at 2662.26510 in the same evaluation. At 02:03:55 this was rejected after 11.85 seconds with `Stop Loss price should be greater than the current price`; the manager recorded a failed replacement. The exit observer later recorded `STOP_MARKET`, trigger basis `MARK_PRICE`, trigger 2673.51, fill 2695.49 at 02:04:12, realized PnL -0.1007 USDT and fees -0.010782 USDT. Gross price move from entry is approximately -4.69% leveraged ROE. The first stop's verified existence is not proof of execution at its trigger price.

**Public-market comparison, not VST proof.** A public unauthenticated BingX `ETH-USDT` USD-M mark-price 1m request for 2026-10-07 02:00–02:07 UTC returned 02:04 high 2615.98 and low 2607.50, well below the VST fill 2695.49. Endpoint: `GET https://open-api.bingx.com/openApi/swap/v1/market/markPriceKlines?symbol=ETH-USDT&interval=1m&startTime=1791338400000&endTime=1791338820000&limit=10`; the corresponding public trade-price kline endpoint also returned 02:04 high 2616.00. These public live-market candles are **not** the VST mark/order book. They establish a material public-vs-VST discrepancy, not whether the demo venue, local price feed, latency, or another cause generated the VST trigger/fill.

**Separate platform provenance defect.** The 2026-10-04 ETH entry `INTENT` in `data/live_order_journal.json` records initial SL 86399.6012 and TP 83838.3482, invalid for ETH near 2682.90; the forward position observer instead had an exchange-side initial stop state of 2751.37. `extract_order_journal_metadata` prioritizes `risk_parameters` over executable `order.stop_loss_price`/`take_profit_price`. A networkless reproduction returned the stale risk-parameter values even when the executable stop was supplied. Therefore the journal fields do **not** prove that the absurd levels were sent to the broker. The mismatch is nevertheless a confirmed attribution/audit defect and must be fixed separately from order policy.

**Task ETH-STOP-P0.** Preserve the raw exit and journal evidence. Reconcile exchange order IDs and the VST stop trigger/fill from already-authorized evidence; do not infer that a trigger guarantees fill price. Instrument or characterize separately: local queue/rate-limit wait, request latency, pending-stop visibility latency, mark-price freshness, and VST-vs-public-market divergence. Add networkless regression tests for a fast adverse move during stop replacement and for journal metadata using the executable protected prices. Do not cancel/replace current orders, loosen stop rules, suppress errors, or assume the VST behavior will match live trading. Any protective-order lifecycle code change requires credential/network isolation tests and independent review before deployment. Acceptance: no false claim of a confirmed protective amendment, no wrong-symbol SL/TP in the journal, and an explicit residual-uncertainty statement about VST fill mechanics.
